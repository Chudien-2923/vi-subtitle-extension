CHUDIN VI SUBTITLE - local Vietnamese voiceover: third-party notices
====================================================================

This file lists the software and model weights downloaded onto the user's machine
when the user installs the optional local voice ("Cai/Tai giong"). Nothing here is
bundled inside the browser extension. Everything is downloaded from its original
publisher at a pinned version and verified by SHA-256 (see asset-manifest.json and
requirements.lock).
